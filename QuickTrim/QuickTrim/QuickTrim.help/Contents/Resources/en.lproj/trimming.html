QuickTrim Help

Trim with regions

Each cut you mark splits the file into regions. Every region is either kept or binned. Only kept regions are exported, in order.

Mark a cut

Move the playhead to the frame where you want to cut, then press M or click Mark Trim. A yellow marker appears on the timeline and the region is split in two.

Bin or keep a region

  • Press B to toggle the region under the playhead between kept and binned.
  • Or click the trash button next to a region in the Regions list.

Binned regions show in red on the timeline and are struck through in the Regions list. Click a region in the list to jump to its start.

Preview what you’ll export

Click Preview or press ⌥⌘P. The timeline collapses to show only kept regions, and playback skips everything you’ve binned. The scissors timecode above the timeline always shows the trimmed length.

Undo and redo

Choose Edit > Undo (⌘Z) or Redo (⇧⌘Z). Marking, binning, moving and removing markers can all be undone. Your undo history is kept after you export, so you can adjust and export again.

Closing with unexported changes

If you’ve made regions that haven’t been exported, QuickTrim asks before closing the window or quitting, so you don’t lose your work by accident.
