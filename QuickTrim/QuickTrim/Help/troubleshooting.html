Troubleshooting

“Could Not Open” when opening a file

The file has no video or audio that macOS can play, or it’s in a format macOS doesn’t support (such as many AVI files). Try converting it to MP4 or MOV with another app first.

I can’t choose WAV when exporting an MP3

Compressed audio such as MP3 can only be exported as M4A. WAV and AIFF are available when the original is WAV or AIFF. See File formats.

An exported file misbehaves in another app

Lossless exports copy the original data exactly. A few apps handle such files poorly at the cut points. Turn on Transcode output in the export options and export again.

Export failed

Check there’s enough free space at the destination and that you’re not exporting over the original file. If it still fails, try Transcode output.

The Export button is dimmed

Every region is binned, so there’s nothing to export. Keep at least one region with B or its trash button.

The waveform is very small or flat

The recording is extremely quiet or silent. The waveform is scaled to each file’s loudest moment, up to a limit, so near-silent files stay flat rather than magnifying noise.
