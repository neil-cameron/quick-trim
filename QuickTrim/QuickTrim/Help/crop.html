Crop video

Cropping removes pixels from the edges of the picture. It applies to the whole exported video.

Crop the picture

  1. Click the Crop button below the picture, or press ⌥⌘C.
  2. Drag the corner or edge handles, or use the Left, Top, Right and Bottom sliders and fields.
  3. Click Done. The picture shows the cropped result.

In a crop field, press ↑ or ↓ to change the value by 1, or hold ⇧ to change it by 10. Press Return to apply or Esc to cancel. Click Reset to remove the crop. The output size is shown beside the controls.

A cropped export has to be re-encoded, so it takes longer than a normal export. Width and height are rounded down to even numbers, which video encoders require.
