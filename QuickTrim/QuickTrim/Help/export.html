Export

Click Export or press ⇧⌘E. QuickTrim joins the kept regions in order and saves them as a new file. Your original file isn’t changed.

Lossless by default

Normally QuickTrim copies the original audio and video data without re-encoding it. That’s fast and keeps the original quality. It re-encodes only when it has to: when you crop, when you turn on Transcode output, or when the original format can’t be copied into the file type you chose.

Export options

Click the gear button beside Export:

No soundExports the video without its audio. Video files only.
Transcode outputRe-encodes instead of copying. Slower, but useful if another app has trouble with a losslessly exported file.

Audio from every track is kept. Recordings with separate microphone and system-audio tracks export with both.

You can’t export over the file you’re trimming. Choose a different name or location.
