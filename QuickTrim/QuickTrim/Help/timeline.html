Use the timeline

Zoom

Zoom from 100% to 5000% with ⌘+ and ⌘−, the magnifying-glass buttons, or the slider. Double-click the percentage to return to 100%. When you zoom, the view stays centred on the playhead. At high zoom the time ruler shows tenths of a second.

Skimming

With Skimming on (⌥⌘S), moving the pointer across the timeline scrubs the picture and plays short snippets of sound, so you can find a spot without pressing Play. Skimming doesn’t interrupt normal playback.

Follow the playhead

Turn on the location-arrow button (⌃⌥⌘S) to scroll the timeline automatically and keep the playhead in view while it plays.

The waveform

For audio files, and for video files that have sound, QuickTrim draws the audio as a continuous waveform. The pale outer shape shows peaks, and the brighter core shows average loudness, so silence and quiet speech are easy to tell apart. Video files show the waveform underneath the frame thumbnails. Each file’s waveform is scaled so its loudest moment fills the available height.
